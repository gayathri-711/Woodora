Artisan Heritage

Devoted to the Art of Honest Furniture Design

WOODORA was founded in 1998 with an uncompromising commitment: to build furniture from genuine, solid timber without shortcuts, particleboard, or toxic finishes.

We partner directly with certified foresters in France, Germany, and North America. Master woodworkers shape each slab using traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery and rub each grain with plant-based hardwax oils that age gracefully over decades.

Two Trees Planted for Every Harvest Active reforestation ensures our natural materials leave the earth richer.
Zero-VOC Organic Finishes Safe for children, pets, and indoor air quality in every sanctuary.
