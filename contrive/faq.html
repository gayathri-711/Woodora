Hardwood Care & Longevity Guide

Handcrafted solid wood develops a richer patina with age. Follow our artisan principles to preserve your investment for generations.

1. Natural Conditioning

Apply organic beeswax or linseed balm once a year. It deeply nourishes the timber grain while preserving natural micro-porosity.

2. Climate & Lighting

Maintain home humidity between 40% and 55%. Avoid direct exposure to prolonged direct heating vents to prevent natural expansion.

3. Surface Protection

Use natural stone or wool felt coasters beneath hot culinary vessels. Wipe accidental spills with a dry microfiber cloth immediately.
