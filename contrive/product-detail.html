Architectural Ergonomics & Hand-Joined White Oak

The Nordic Minimalist Oak Sofa embodies the essence of the WOODORA ethos: organic materials treated with reverence, pared down to clean architectural lines that bring tranquility into your space.

Caring for Solid Oak & Bouclé

Solid wood breathes with your home’s seasons. To maintain its lustrous patina:

  • Wipe wooden frames with a dry or lightly dampened microfiber cloth. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners.
  • Vacuum bouclé fabric regularly using a soft brush attachment.
  • Rotate and fluff cushions monthly to preserve down loft and uniform softness.
  • We recommend applying WOODORA organic furniture wax once a year to nourish the natural timber.
