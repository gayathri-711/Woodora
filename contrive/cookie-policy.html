Use of Cookies & Session Storage

WOODORA uses lightweight cookies and browser storage technologies strictly to deliver an effortless, elevated shopping experience.

1. Essential Shopping Cookies

These storage tokens are required for core operations:

  • Cart Retention (woodora_cart_items): Preserves your curated furniture pieces, chosen upholstery colors, and quantities as you navigate between pages.
  • Wishlist Persistence (woodora_wishlist_items): Keeps your saved architectural items securely in memory.
  • Authentication Session (woodora_active_user): Maintains your active signed-in member status across checkout and catalogue pages.
  • Coupon Privilege (woodora_applied_coupon): Remembers valid promotional discount codes like WOODORA10.

2. Analytics & Performance

We observe anonymized traffic trends to optimize product load times, enhance mobile rendering performance, and understand which furniture categories receive the highest architectural interest.

3. Managing Your Browser Preferences

You may disable cookies at any time via your browser settings. Please note that disabling session storage will prevent the shopping cart from retaining chosen products during multi-page browsing.
